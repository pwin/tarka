These files are copied unchanged from the Semantechs courses, so that the manual's examples stand
on their own:

- `01-vocabulary.ttl` to `09-stock.ttl`: the Bookshop Trail dataset and its ontology, from the
  [Ontology course](https://github.com/pwin/Ontology_Course) (`data/`), which has it from the
  [SPARQL course](https://github.com/pwin/SPARQL_Course).
- `s64-the-whole-trail-in-one-shapes-graph.ttl`: from the
  [SHACL course](https://github.com/pwin/SHACL_Course) (`shapes/11-putting-it-together/`).
- `q01-every-bookshop-on-the-trail.rq`: from the SPARQL course (`queries/01-first-queries/`).

coincidental. See the [SPARQL course's NOTICE](https://github.com/pwin/SPARQL_Course/blob/main/NOTICE.md).
